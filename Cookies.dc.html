This Cookie Policy explains how Second Opinion Radiology uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. By continuing to use our site, you agree to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, as well as to provide information to website owners.

Types of cookies we use

TypeWhat it does
EssentialNecessary for the website to function properly — page navigation, secure areas and form submissions. These cookies do not require your consent.
Performance & analyticsTools such as Google Analytics collect information on how visitors use the site, so we can improve performance and user experience. All data collected is anonymised.
FunctionalityAllow the website to remember your preferences, such as region or language, and provide enhanced features.
Targeting & advertisingWith your consent, tools such as Facebook Pixel or Google Ads cookies deliver relevant advertising and measure campaign effectiveness. These cookies track browsing behaviour across websites.

Cookie control and consent

When you first visit our site, you are shown a cookie banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by adjusting the settings in your browser, or by using our cookie settings tool where available.

Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services on our website, such as embedded video players or social media links. We do not control the use of these cookies. Please refer to the respective third party’s privacy and cookie policies.

Data protection

For more information about how we use your personal data, including information collected through cookies, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
