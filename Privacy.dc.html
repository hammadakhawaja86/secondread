What does Second Opinion Radiology do?

We provide second opinion radiology services on scans such as MRI, CT and X-rays. Radiologists review medical imaging to support clinical decisions by patients, referring doctors, legal teams or insurers.

  • The referring clinician or patient submits the imaging.
  • We receive imaging data and relevant clinical history through secure upload systems.
  • A GMC-registered radiologist reviews the scan and generates a structured diagnostic report.
  • The report is securely returned to the referrer or patient.

Our responsibilities

Second Opinion Radiology as data processor. When we provide reports on behalf of hospitals, clinics or healthcare partners, we act as a data processor and the referring provider remains the data controller. Data we process may include:

  • Patient demographics (name, date of birth, NHS number and similar)
  • Referral information (clinical history)
  • Medical imaging (for example MRI, CT and X-ray scans)

We only process personal information as instructed by the data controller, or where legally required.

Second Opinion Radiology as data controller. We may act as a data controller for certain activities, including maintaining professional or regulatory records; handling complaints and legal matters; record-keeping obligations under Care Quality Commission rules; and internal clinical audit, regulatory compliance and governance.

Sharing personal information

Your data may be shared with:

Automated decision-making

Second Opinion Radiology does not use automated decision-making in clinical or customer service processes. Website-related features may use anonymised data for optimisation.

Data retention

We retain clinical and business data according to legal and regulatory requirements. Imaging is typically held securely for a defined period and archived for medico-legal purposes in line with NHS and professional guidelines.

Data security

We apply robust technical and organisational measures, including:

  • Encrypted data transfer
  • Secure cloud-based storage
  • Regular penetration testing
  • Confidentiality contracts for staff and radiologists
  • Access control and audit trails

All personal data is processed in compliance with UK GDPR and relevant NHS guidance.

Your rights

Under UK data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request erasure, under certain conditions
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

To exercise your rights, contact info@secondopinionradiology.co.uk. We will respond within the statutory time frame and may request identity verification.
