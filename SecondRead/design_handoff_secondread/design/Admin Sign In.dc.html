Internal console

Where a case
finds its radiologist.

Assignment, review, sign-off and release, the working side of Second Opinion Radiology, for the people who run it.

OperationsAssign a case, chase a clock, handle an exception.
RadiologistsRead the cases offered to you, draft and sign the report.
Clinical leadSecond-read sign-off and urgent-finding escalation.

Access is granted by an administrator, never self-service. Every sign-in, every case opened and every report signed is logged against your name.

Sign in

Use the work address your account was created with. We will email you a six-digit code.

Personal addresses are not accepted. If you have changed address, an administrator has to move your account first.

Why no password

Second Opinion Radiology has no passwords, on either side of the service. A code that expires in ten minutes cannot be reused, shared or left in a browser on a ward computer.

Cannot get in? Email info@secondopinionradiology.co.uk and an administrator will check your account.
