Medical Disclaimer

What a second opinion from us is for, what it cannot do, and where clinical responsibility sits.

Reviewed 5 October 2026
If this is an emergency

This service is not for emergencies and we cannot respond to one. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services on 999 or go to your nearest hospital immediately. For urgent advice that is not an emergency, call NHS 111.

Reproduced from Second Opinion Radiology’s published medical disclaimer.

The information and radiology reports provided by Second Opinion Radiology are intended for informational and advisory purposes only. Our service offers second opinions by UK-registered radiologists, based on the imaging and clinical data provided by the patient or their referring clinician.

Not a substitute for primary or emergency care

Our reports do not replace the role of your primary treating doctor or specialist. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease independently. Second Opinion Radiology does not assume clinical responsibility for the ongoing care of the patient.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, you must contact emergency services or visit the nearest hospital immediately.

Scope of advice

Reports issued are based solely on the scans and information submitted. Without access to the full clinical picture, physical examination, or patient history beyond what is submitted, the advice may be limited in scope. All findings and recommendations should be reviewed and discussed with your treating physician.

Patient responsibility

By using our services, you confirm that:

  • You understand the second opinion is supplemental, not a replacement for direct clinical care.
  • You accept that all healthcare decisions remain your responsibility and/or that of your primary physician.

Questions

If you have any questions about this disclaimer or your report, please contact our clinical team at info@secondopinionradiology.co.uk, or write to 188–189 Drury Lane, London, WC2B 5QD, United Kingdom.
