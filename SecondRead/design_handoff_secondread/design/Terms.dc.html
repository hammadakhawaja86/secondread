Terms

The agreement between you and SecondRead Ltd — what we will do, what we will not do, and where you stand if something goes wrong.

What SecondRead is, and what it is not

SecondRead provides an independent radiological opinion on imaging you already have. A GMC-registered consultant radiologist reviews your study and writes a report.

This is not an emergency service, and it is not a substitute for seeing a doctor. We interpret images. We do not examine you, we do not diagnose you, we do not prescribe, and we do not take over your care from the clinicians treating you. Our report is written to inform the conversation you have with them.

If you are acutely unwell, in severe pain, or your symptoms are getting worse, contact 111, your GP, or 999. Do not wait for a report from us.

Who can use the service

You must be 18 or over to place an order. You may order on behalf of a child in your care, or on behalf of an adult who has given you authority to do so, and by ordering you confirm that you have that authority.

You must have the right to share the images you send us. In practice this means they are your own images, or you have the consent of the person they belong to.

Your order

A contract forms when we confirm your order and take payment, not when you upload your images.

The turnaround you select is a deadline for the signed report, measured from the point your payment clears and we have a complete, readable study. If a series is missing or an image will not open, we will contact you and the clock pauses until we have what we need.

We will name your reporting consultant, with their hospital and GMC number, before you pay. If that consultant becomes unavailable we will tell you and offer you an equally qualified subspecialist, a different deadline, or a full refund — your choice.

Our deadline guarantee

If we miss the deadline you paid for, we refund your fee in full. You do not have to ask and you do not have to argue about it — the refund is issued automatically and you keep the report.

The guarantee does not apply where the delay is caused by images we could not read, information we asked you for and did not receive, or a request from you to change the study or the consultant after the order was placed.

Cancellation and refunds

You have the statutory right to cancel a distance contract within 14 days. Because a radiology report is bespoke and begins immediately, that right ends once the consultant has started reading your study — and by placing an order you agree to us starting straight away.

In practice: cancel before your study is opened and you get a full refund, no questions. Cancel after the consultant has begun and we will refund the portion of the work not yet done. Once the report is signed, the fee is not refundable except under the deadline guarantee above.

What we charge

Prices are shown on the Pricing page and confirmed before you pay. The price you see is the price you pay — there are no consultation fees, admin charges or per-image extras.

Add-ons such as written follow-up questions or a video call are priced separately and clearly at checkout.

Complaints and clinical concerns

If you are unhappy with your report, tell us. Email info@secondread.co.uk and we will acknowledge within two working days and respond substantively within twenty.

Where a complaint concerns clinical judgement, we will arrange a review by a second consultant of equal seniority at no cost to you.

Nothing in these terms affects your right to raise a concern about a doctor directly with the General Medical Council, or to pursue any other remedy available to you in law.

Our liability

We are liable to you for losses caused by our failure to use reasonable care and skill in providing the service.

We do not exclude or limit our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be excluded — and we make no attempt to.

Our report is an opinion on the images supplied. It cannot account for clinical findings we were not given, symptoms we were not told about, or imaging you did not send us.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland you may also bring proceedings in your own courts.
