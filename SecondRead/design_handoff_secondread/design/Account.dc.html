Privacy & your data

Your images and reports are held in the UK, encrypted, and never sent in an email or shown in a notification. Every time anyone opens your case — our team, your consultant, or a doctor you shared it with — it is logged, and you can ask to see that log.

What we send you, and what it says

One message per change of status. None of them names your scan, the part of your body, or anything your consultant found — not in the subject line, not in the preview, and not on a locked screen. To read anything, you sign in.
