Accessibility Statement

How we try to make this site usable for everyone, what still falls short, and how to tell us.

Reviewed 5 October 2026

Reproduced from Second Opinion Radiology’s published accessibility statement.

Second Opinion Radiology is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including people with disabilities. We are continually working to improve the user experience and apply the relevant accessibility standards wherever possible.

Commitment to accessibility

We aim for our website to be accessible and usable for everyone, including people who:

  • use screen readers or keyboard navigation
  • have visual, hearing, motor or cognitive impairments
  • rely on voice commands or screen magnification tools

The website is designed to meet at least WCAG 2.1 AA standards as far as reasonably possible.

Features in place

  • Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Sufficient colour contrast and readable font sizing
  • Clear navigation and consistent layout
  • Alt text for images wherever appropriate
  • Headings and structure to support screen readers
  • Keyboard-accessible forms and buttons

Known limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some older PDF files or third-party integrations, for example embedded booking tools or AI imaging portals, may not meet full accessibility standards. We are actively working with our providers to address this.
