Terms & Conditions

What our service is, what it is not, and the terms you accept when you ask us to read a scan.

Reproduced from Second Opinion Radiology’s published terms. Company: CYA Medical Ltd, trading as Second Opinion Radiology.

Nature of the service

  • Second Opinion Radiology provides expert radiological opinions on imaging studies.
  • Our reports are advisory and do not replace the original imaging report issued by the provider who performed your scan.
  • The final clinical diagnosis and management decisions remain the responsibility of your treating doctor or specialist.

Scope of reporting

  • Reports are based solely on the imaging provided to us. We do not have access to your full medical records, blood tests, examination findings or clinical notes.
  • Our radiologists will interpret the images as submitted. Any missing or incomplete clinical information may affect the report.
  • Explanatory sections, such as “What this means for you”, are for patient understanding only and are not intended for treating clinicians.

Fees and payment

  • All fees must be paid in full before a report is issued.
  • Fees are calculated according to the number and type of imaging studies submitted. The standard fee is £250. Where comparison studies are included, a further £50 applies, making £300 in total.
  • No refunds will be given once reporting has commenced, irrespective of outcome or patient satisfaction.

Limitations and outcomes

  • A second opinion may or may not differ from your original report. It may or may not change your clinical management.
  • Second Opinion Radiology cannot guarantee that our interpretation will alter the course of your treatment.
  • Differences between reports do not imply error or negligence; radiology involves professional judgement, and reasonable differences of opinion can occur.

Amendments to reports

  • The factual radiological findings represent the independent, professional interpretation of the reporting consultant and cannot be altered at a patient’s request.
  • Typographical or clerical errors will be corrected if identified.
  • Non-clinical explanatory sections may be adapted or removed if requested by the patient.

Liability

  • Our reports are prepared with due professional care by UK-registered consultant radiologists.
  • We are not responsible for any decisions made by treating clinicians based on our reports, nor for outcomes of treatment.
  • By engaging our service, you accept that Second Opinion Radiology is not liable for direct or indirect consequences of medical or surgical decisions taken by others.

Confidentiality

All personal and medical information provided will be handled securely and in accordance with GDPR and UK data protection law. See our Privacy Policy for the detail.

Intellectual property

Reports provided remain the intellectual property of Second Opinion Radiology and must not be altered or redistributed without permission.

Governing law

These Terms & Conditions are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
